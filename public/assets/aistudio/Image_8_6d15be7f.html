Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How does 24/7 RFID Turnstile entry work? expand_more

Once enrolled, your digital pass is instantly synced to the Warrior Companion web app. You can tap your smartphone at the turnstile NFC receiver, or collect an RFID key fob at the reception desk during staffed hours.

Can I freeze or pause my membership during travel? expand_more

Yes. 3-Month, 6-Month, and 12-Month tiers allow up to 30 days of complimentary membership pause through your member portal profile without documentation.

Are cold plunge and infrared sauna sessions included? expand_more

The Recovery Suite is fully included in the Premium Conditioning (6-Month) and Annual VIP Elite plans. Basic and Standard members can purchase day passes for ₹150 per session.

What is the 3-Day Trial Pass policy? expand_more

First-time visitors can claim a 3-Day Pass online. It provides unrestricted access during staffed hours (5:00 AM – 11:30 PM) including all group conditioning classes and orientation sessions.
